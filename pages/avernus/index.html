The campaign in brief

Elturel has disappeared into Avernus, an infernal realm. The characters investigate its disappearance and seek a way to reach the city and help its inhabitants. Missing relatives, inherited pacts and questions about identity give them personal reasons to pursue that investigation.

My role and contribution

I ran and adapted Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus for my group. The published adventure supplies the setting, central conflict and many playable encounters. Player backgrounds supply the personal premises; my adaptations connect those premises to NPCs, clues and campaign objectives.

The case studies compare the preparation with attributed player recaps. They distinguish material that was played from content that remains planned.
