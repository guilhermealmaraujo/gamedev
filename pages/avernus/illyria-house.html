Illyria's house:
personal discoveries,
a shared investigation.

I used a recurring NPC's home to connect character backgrounds with discoverable clues, individual interactions and a shared revelation about the campaign's wider conflict.

Role: Dungeon Master & campaign adapterFormat: tabletop RPGEvidence: preparation & attributed player recaps
CORE COMPETENCY

Narrative into interaction

Personal mysteries become objects to examine, questions to ask and discoveries to investigate.

SUPPORTING COMPETENCY

Character motivations

Each interaction starts from a concern already present in the character's background.

SUPPORTING COMPETENCY

Connected content

01 / CONTEXT & DESIGN CHALLENGE

What you need to know

Elturel, a city connected to the characters' families and mentors, has disappeared into Avernus, an infernal realm. The group is investigating what happened and seeking a way to reach the city and help its inhabitants.

During the investigation in Baldur's Gate, they visit Illyria, Eric's mentor and a scholar of planar magic. Her home contains knowledge and objects related to their personal mysteries.

Eric
Searching for his missing mother; Illyria knows his family.
Eres
Rejected by a family of mages; seeking an explanation for his inability to use magic.
Laphi
Investigating lost fire knowledge and the grimoire inherited from his mother.
Vera
Seeking to understand her origin and her absent vampire mother's history.
Elentir
Searching for missing relatives and investigating a pact associated with his clan's weapons.

Different reasons to visit the same place.

Eric knows Illyria through his background. Eres has been directed toward her through his mentor's research. Vera is investigating her family, while Laphi is pursuing the Black Flame myth. The preparation brings these concerns into the same house, where books, portraits and magical instruments provide different ways to engage.

The scene illustrates how a shared location can offer different reasons to participate while connecting personal discoveries to the campaign investigation.

Scope of this case: four personal interactions inside the house, a related encounter for Elentir on the approach, and the vision experienced by the group. Elentir's approach encounter is distinct from the indoor interactions. Later visits and Seraphiel's involvement are not established by this scene packet.

03 / WHAT THE DESIGN DEMONSTRATES

The decisions behind the scene.

Use the location to deliver clues

A portrait, a library and a diagnostic instrument carry different information. Characters can encounter it through looking, researching and asking for help. This demonstrates narrative interaction design: information is attached to an action and a setting feature.

Give characters reasons to participate

The information relates to concerns in the backgrounds: family, magical ability and inherited knowledge. This demonstrates the use of character motivations to shape encounter content. It does not establish equal participation time or engagement for every player.

Connect a personal object to the group plot

Eric's amulet has personal significance, but the vision is experienced by the group. This demonstrates a connection between a character arc and shared campaign information, rather than a separate biography presented outside play.

Document conditions for information access

The preparation specifies interaction triggers and conditional answers, including questions about the library's provenance. This demonstrates runnable content documentation. The house is not presented here as a fully branching quest with multiple endings.

06 / REFLECTION

What I would evaluate in a revision.

As a current design review, I would examine whether each clue gave the group a useful next question, whether the shared vision delivered more information than players could act on, and whether conditional discoveries were missed. These are proposed evaluation questions, not claims about revisions already made.

INFORMATION LOAD

Several mysteries at once

Separate the essential campaign information from optional detail and make the key discoveries easy to revisit.

PARTICIPATION

Who acts, who watches?

Examine the opportunities to act in each interaction and the largely observational historical vision.

MISSED CLUES

Preparation is not delivery

Check whether the portrait was discovered and whether another route to that information was available.

07 / SOURCES & CONTRIBUTION

What belongs to this adaptation.

The published adventure supplies the setting, central conflict, Lulu and substantial playable content. Player backgrounds supply the personal premises. The house preparation connects those premises through specific objects, NPC interactions and a shared vision.

The scene packet combines DM preparation, attributed player recaps, published material and ChatGPT-assisted drafting. This case analyzes interaction design; the historical vision and literary reconstruction are not presented as wholly original lore or verbatim session records.
