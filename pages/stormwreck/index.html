What you need to know

Stormwreck Isle is an island marked by ancient dragon conflicts. Dragon remains, volcanic activity and magical disturbances provide a premise for exploration. This project expands that premise into travel decisions, regional hazards and encounters that communicate the island's history.

The design uses the setting of the published D&D adventure Dragons of Stormwreck Isle. The portfolio focuses on the developing exploration specification and its intended connection to a digital prototype.

Current scope

Version 1.2 provides substantial travel and hazard rules, encounter-generator structures and initial regional tables. It also identifies future content, including climate and rewards. Some definitions, tables and examples remain incomplete or inconsistent.

The Unreal project is at an early stage. Documented mechanics are design specifications, not evidence of engine implementation or successful playtesting.
