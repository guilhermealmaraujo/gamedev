STORMWRECK · SYSTEMS DESIGN & UNREAL

Make the journey a player decision.

An exploration system connecting travel speed, terrain hazards, party activities, and encounters grounded in the island's history.

Role: systems designer & prototype developerStatus: in developmentTools: Unreal Engine · C++ · Blueprints
DESIGN & IMPLEMENTATION

The exploration problem

The design aims to make travel a sequence of choices: where to go, how quickly to move, and how to divide the group's attention between navigation, safety, and discovery.

01 / CHOOSERoute & speed

Terrain and travel pace shape the risks ahead.

02 / PREPAREParty activities

Characters allocate attention and support.

03 / RESOLVEConsequences

Resolve hazards and discover narrative encounters.

Encounters with context

The encounter generator combines terrain-specific creatures, their behaviour, and draconic context. The goal is to create situations players can interpret and respond to.

From rules to a prototype

The Unreal prototype is being developed around a hex-based travel map and explorable locations. A future implementation case study will pair design decisions with a recorded demonstration and selected code.

The documented system and the implemented prototype will be presented separately, with their current scope made explicit.
